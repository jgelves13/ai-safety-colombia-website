What it is

Apart Research brings AI safety to the Global South. Researchers, engineers, and policy professionals from Latin America, Africa, and Asia tackle AI risks relevant to their regions over a weekend. AI Safety Colombia runs the Bogotá hub.

Why take part

AI safety research is still concentrated in a handful of countries and its fine-tuning is done mostly in English. In controlled tests, translating harmful prompts into lower-resource languages defeated GPT-4's safeguards in roughly 79% of cases. Meanwhile, systems are already being deployed across the region with no local audit: the algorithm the Salta provincial government used to predict which teenage girls would become pregnant from invasive proxies, and Colombia's SISBÉN shift to a model that predicts income-generation capacity, whose code was not released to the public. This is why Apart Research is bringing AI safety to the Global South, and this hackathon is the way in: over one weekend you build evaluations, tools or policy research on these problems and step into the Apart pipeline, where winning teams are invited to the Apart Lab Fellowship, publish at NeurIPS and ICLR workshops, and move into roles at AI safety organizations.

Tracks

Technical AI Safety

Understand and verify how models behave inside: how agentic systems evaluate, plan and act, and how to inspect what they learn.

  • Evaluations for agentic systems
  • Mechanistic interpretability

AI Security

Protect AI systems against misuse and attacks: secure deployment pipelines, resist prompt injection and jailbreaks, and keep control over capable models.

  • Pipeline security (API/Cloud)
  • Prompt injection and jailbreaks
  • AI control

Responsible AI

Reduce harm in real-world use: mitigate hallucinations, audit model behavior across languages and cultures, and evaluate social impact.

  • Hallucination mitigation
  • Behavioral audit (multilingual, cross-cultural)
  • Social impact evaluation

AI Governance

The rules and institutions that steer AI: policy and regulation analysis, auditing and accountability, ecosystem monitoring, and lethal autonomous weapons, a focus area led from Bogotá.

  • AI policy and regulation analysis
  • System auditing and accountability
  • Ecosystem monitoring
  • Lethal autonomous weapons systems

The prize is more than the money. Winning teams from past Apart sprints have been invited to the Apart Lab Fellowship, where weekend prototypes have grown into expert-reviewed research, been presented at machine-learning conference workshops, and led to roles at AI safety organizations.

Real results from past sprints

  • Apart fellows' work has been accepted at leading machine-learning conferences including NeurIPS, ICLR, ACL and EMNLP.
  • Participants have moved from non-research roles into full-time AI safety positions, such as research engineer at CAIF and research lead at Martian.
  • A collaboration with METR produced 230 evaluation ideas and 28 implementations in a single hackathon of 100+ participants.
  • Over 3,000 people have taken part in Apart sprints, producing hundreds of research reports and dozens of AI safety publications.
